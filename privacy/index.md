# Vyan AI 隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

## 繁體中文

**生效日期：2026 年 11 月 11 日**

Vyan AI（以下稱「本 App」）是一款陪伴與情緒照顧 App。我們相信你在最脆弱的時候寫下的東西，只屬於你自己。這份政策說明本 App 會處理哪些資料、存在哪裡，以及你可以怎麼刪除它們。

### 一句話版本

你的日記、心情、評量、健康資料和你跟夥伴說的話，**都只存在你的裝置上，我們看不到，也不會傳到任何伺服器**。會離開你裝置的只有三樣：訂閱付款相關的紀錄，由 RevenueCat 處理，而且不會連結到你的身分；你自己打開「天氣共感」時，送給 Apple 查天氣用的大約位置（約 10 公里內）；以及你自己在星星商店按下「看一支短廣告」時，Google AdMob 為了播出那支廣告而收到的裝置與網路資訊（不個人化、不追蹤，見第 2d 節）。面部跟隨若你打開，臉只在這台裝置上用來轉動畫面上的夥伴，不保存、不上傳。如果你打開好友聊天的「即時送達」，你傳給朋友的訊息和照片會先在你的手機上鎖好，再經過 iCloud 送到朋友手上，只有你們兩人打得開，我們沒有伺服器，也看不到內容（見第 2e 節）。

### 1. 我們不收集的資料

本 App 沒有帳號系統，不需要註冊，也不會要求你的姓名、Email 或電話。以下資料**只存在你的裝置上**，開發者無法取得：

- **日記、心情打卡、評量結果**（例如 PHQ-9、GAD-7）、遊戲與練習紀錄。
- **你和夥伴的對話內容。** 夥伴的回應在支援 Apple Intelligence 的裝置上，由 Apple 的裝置端模型在你的手機裡產生，文字不會送到網路上；其他裝置則使用 App 內建的文字。
- **危機偵測。** 當你寫下的文字可能代表你正處於危險中，本 App 會在裝置上判斷，並顯示求助資源。這個判斷完全在你的手機上完成，**不會通知任何人，也不會傳送到任何地方**。
- **健康資料。** 經你同意後，本 App 會透過 Apple「健康」讀取心率、心率變異、靜息心率、呼吸速率、睡眠（含深層與快速動眼階段）、步數與經期紀錄，用來呈現你自己的長期變化；也可能把呼吸練習寫入「健康」成為正念分鐘數。這些是數值與時間紀錄，不是醫療檢測：呼吸速率是手錶寫下的每分鐘次數，本 App 不會據此判讀呼吸波形、呼吸中止或任何疾病。健康資料**不會離開你的裝置、不會用於廣告或行銷、不會出售，也不會提供給任何第三方**。你可以隨時在 iPhone「設定 ▸ 健康 ▸ 資料取用權限與裝置」關閉。
- **你信任的人。** 你在 App 裡填寫的聯絡人姓名與電話只存在你的裝置上，只有在你自己按下撥打時才會透過 iPhone 撥號。
- **好友邀請。** 好友邀請是一條你自己透過系統分享（例如 iMessage、LINE）傳出去的連結，內容只有你選擇顯示的名字、你的內在小孩、一組隨機邀請碼，以及一把聊天用的「公開鑰匙」（只能用來把訊息鎖給你，不能用來打開任何東西）。本 App 沒有伺服器，不會經手或保存這條連結。
- **密碼鎖。** 你設的 PIN 碼以雜湊存在這台裝置的鑰匙圈，開發者看不到，也不會上傳。Face ID 或觸控 ID 只在你這台裝置上驗證。
- **通知。** 每日訊息等提醒都是在你的裝置上排程的本機通知，不經過任何伺服器。
- **Apple Watch 與小工具。** 手機和手錶之間的資料透過 Apple 的 WatchConnectivity 直接傳輸；主畫面小工具只讀取同一台裝置上的資料。兩者都不會收集任何資料。

除了第 2e 節你自己打開的好友聊天「即時送達」，本 App 不使用 iCloud 同步。如果你開啟了 iPhone 的 iCloud 備份，或是用電腦備份 iPhone，本 App 在裝置上的資料會跟其他 App 一樣被包含在**你自己的**備份裡，這份備份由 Apple 依你的帳號設定保管，開發者無法取得。

### 2. 我們收集的資料：訂閱付款紀錄

本 App 的付費方案透過 Apple App Store 付款，並使用第三方服務 **RevenueCat, Inc.** 管理訂閱狀態。RevenueCat 會處理：

- 一組**隨機產生的匿名 ID**（不是你的姓名、Email 或 Apple ID）；
- 你購買的方案、購買與到期時間、續訂或退款狀態、App Store 所在國家或地區與幣別；
- 維持服務所需的技術資訊，例如 App 版本、裝置系統版本，以及連線時的 IP 位址。

這些資料的用途**只有**：確認你是否已訂閱、解鎖付費功能、恢復購買，以及了解整體訂閱狀況（例如有多少人訂閱）。這些資料**不會連結到你的身分，不會用於追蹤你，也不會用於廣告**。

付款本身由 Apple 處理，我們和 RevenueCat 都**看不到**你的信用卡或付款資訊。RevenueCat 的資料處理方式請見其隱私權政策：https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

### 2b. 天氣共感（預設關閉）

「天氣共感」讓夥伴知道外面的天氣，例如下雨時窗外會有雨痕、轉冷時會提醒你。這項功能預設是關閉的；關閉時，本 App 不會取得你的位置，也不會查天氣。

- **什麼時候會送出：** 只有在你到「設定 ▸ 天氣共感」選了「大略位置」或一個城市之後。
- **送出什麼：** 選「大略位置」時，經你同意，本 App 向 iOS 要一次**大約位置**（不是精確位置），並在手機上先四捨五入到約 10 公里，才送出；原始座標不會離開手機，也不會被存下來。選城市時，只送出那座城市市中心的座標。
- **誰會處理：** 本 App 透過 iOS 內建的 **Apple WeatherKit** 直接向 Apple 查詢天氣，這項查詢**不經過我們**，我們也看不到。Apple 如何處理這項請求，依 Apple 的隱私權政策。天氣資料的來源清單見 https://developer.apple.com/weatherkit/data-source-attribution/
- **保存：** 你的手機上只會留下最近一次的天氣結果（天氣狀況、氣溫、預報，不含位置）。把「天氣共感」關閉時會一起刪除。

你也可以在 iPhone「設定 ▸ 隱私權與安全性 ▸ 定位服務」隨時收回定位權限。

### 2c. 面部跟隨（你打開才會用鏡頭）

「面部跟隨」用前置鏡頭（TrueDepth）讀你的臉，讓畫面上的夥伴跟著轉頭、眨眼、張嘴。這項功能只有在你進入該畫面時才會開鏡頭。

- **讀什麼：** 這一瞬間的臉部動作（轉頭角度、眨眼、張嘴），只在記憶體裡換成角色的骨骼參數。
- **不保存、不上傳：** 臉部資料不會寫進日記、不會進備份、不會送到任何伺服器。關掉該畫面，鏡頭立刻停。
- **誰處理：** 全部在你的 iPhone 上，透過 Apple 的 ARKit。開發者看不到你的臉。

你可以在 iPhone「設定 ▸ 隱私權與安全性 ▸ 相機」隨時收回鏡頭權限。

### 2d. 看廣告領星星幣（你按了才會播）

星星商店裡有一顆「看一支短廣告，領 10 枚星星幣」的按鈕。廣告由 **Google AdMob**（Google LLC）提供。

- **什麼時候才會連到 Google：** 只有你按下那顆按鈕的時候。從來沒按過的人，本 App 不會連到 Google 的廣告伺服器。
- **Google 會收到什麼：** 為了播出並計算那一支廣告，Google 的廣告程式會收到裝置與網路資訊，例如 IP 位址、裝置型號與系統版本、廣告的播放與點擊紀錄、當機與效能紀錄。這些由 Google 處理，詳見 Google 的說明：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- **不個人化、不追蹤：** 我們要求 Google 只播**不個人化**的廣告，並把廣告內容限制在**普遍級**。本 App **不會**要求「允許追蹤」權限，所以 Google 拿不到你的廣告識別碼。
- **Google 收不到的：** 你的日記、心情、評量、健康資料、和夥伴說的話、記憶，**都不會**放進廣告請求，也不會用來挑選廣告。
- **誰看得到廣告：** 需要先填出生年份（只有年份，只存在你的裝置上）；**未滿 18 歲不會看到任何廣告**。歐洲經濟區、英國、瑞士不提供這項功能。你當天的心情點了「低落」，或當天打開過求助頁，那一天也不會出現廣告。
- **次數：** 一天最多 3 支，兩支之間至少隔 5 分鐘。廣告一定要你自己按才會播，不會自己跳出來。

### 2e. 好友聊天的即時送達（預設關閉）

好友聊天平常是一條你自己分享出去的連結。打開「即時送達」後，訊息、禮物和照片改經過 **Apple 的 iCloud** 直接送到朋友手上，不經過我們，因為本 App 沒有伺服器。

- **先在手機上鎖好：** 每支手機有一把只存在自己鑰匙圈裡的鑰匙，不進 iCloud、不進備份。你和朋友交換過邀請或訊息之後，兩支手機各自算出同一把「兩人鑰匙」。訊息文字、你的名字、內在小孩的名字和照片，都用它在手機上鎖好才上傳。**我們和 Apple 都打不開。**
- **放在哪裡：** 鎖好的訊息放進本 App 在 iCloud 公用資料庫裡的一個「信箱」，所以**不佔你的 iCloud 空間**，你的 iCloud 滿了也照樣送得出去。信箱的編號由你們的兩人鑰匙算出來，只有你們兩人找得到；就算有人拿到，也只是一包打不開的資料。信最多放 14 天，之後由寄件人的 App 收回。
- **門鈴紙條：** 為了讓朋友也拿到你的公開鑰匙，App 會在公用資料庫留一張「門鈴紙條」，寫著你的邀請碼、名字和公開鑰匙，用朋友的公開鑰匙鎖起來，只有他打得開；紙條的位置由他的公開鑰匙算出，沒有拿過他邀請連結的人找不到。
- **沒有鎖的部分：** 信箱裡每封信看得到的只有信箱編號和送出時間，用來找信。誰寄的、寫了什麼、禮物、照片和內在小孩，全部都在鎖好的那一包裡。
- **還沒交換過鑰匙的朋友：** 舊版 App 的朋友沒有公開鑰匙時，訊息改放在你自己 iCloud 裡的一個「寄件匣」，只分享給那一位朋友；文字放在 iCloud 本身的加密欄位，由 Apple 依你的 iCloud 設定加密保管，邀請碼、時間、禮物種類和內在小孩的動物種類沒有加密。這條路會用到你自己的 iCloud 空間。照片一律要等交換過鑰匙才會送出。
- **照片：** 送出前會在手機上縮小並重新壓縮，拍攝地點、相機與拍攝時間等資料都不會帶過去。收到的照片只存在這台裝置上，不進備份。
- **通知：** 新訊息到了的通知只寫「有朋友傳來一則訊息」，不顯示是誰、寫了什麼。
- **關掉或刪除：** 關掉「即時送達」、封鎖一位朋友，或「刪除我的一切」時，App 會收回你放在信箱裡的信、你的寄件匣和你留的門鈴紙條；朋友手機上已經收到的訊息，留在他那一邊。「刪除我的一切」也會換掉這支手機的聊天鑰匙。

### 3. 追蹤與廣告

除了第 2d 節你自己按下才會播的獎勵廣告，本 App **沒有其他廣告**，**不使用**分析工具，**不會**跨 App 或網站追蹤你，也**不會**要求「允許追蹤」權限。我們不出售任何資料。

### 4. 刪除你的資料

- **刪除 App 內的資料：** 在 App 的「設定 ▸ 我的資料 ▸ 刪除我的一切」，可以刪除本 App 存在這台裝置上的所有內容，包括日記、心情、評量、記憶、聊天紀錄與照片、出生年份與設定，並收回你在 iCloud 的聊天信件、寄件匣與門鈴紙條（第 2e 節）。這個動作無法復原。
- **廣告資料：** Google 在播放廣告時收到的資料由 Google 保存與處理；你可以依 Google 的隱私權政策（https://policies.google.com/privacy）向 Google 要求查詢或刪除。
- **刪除 App：** 刪除本 App 也會一併刪除它存在裝置上的所有資料。
- **健康資料：** 寫入 Apple「健康」的正念分鐘數屬於「健康」App，請在「健康」App 中管理或刪除。
- **訂閱紀錄：** 刪除資料不會自動取消訂閱。取消訂閱請到 iPhone「設定 ▸ \[你的名字\] ▸ 訂閱項目」。若要刪除 RevenueCat 保存的匿名購買紀錄，請寫信到下方聯絡信箱；因為紀錄是匿名的，我們可能需要你提供 App Store 收據上的訂單編號來找到它。我們會在 30 天內處理。

## English

**Effective date: November 11, 2026**

Vyan AI ("the App") is a companion and emotional wellbeing app. We believe what you write in your hardest moments belongs to you alone. This policy explains what data the App handles, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

### The short version

Your journal, moods, questionnaires, health data and what you say to your companion **stay on your device. We cannot see them, and they are never sent to any server.** Only three things leave your device: your subscription purchase record, which is handled by RevenueCat and is not linked to your identity; if you turn on Weather yourself, an approximate location (within about 10 km) sent to Apple to look up the weather; and, if you tap "Watch a short ad" in the star shop yourself, the device and network information Google AdMob receives to play that ad (non-personalized and not used for tracking — see section 2d). If you turn on Face follow, your face is used only on this device to turn the on-screen companion. It is not saved and not sent anywhere. If you turn on instant delivery for friend chat, the messages and photos you send are locked on your phone first and then travel through iCloud to your friend. Only the two of you can open them; we have no server and cannot see them (see section 2e).

### 1. Data we do not collect

The App has no accounts. You do not sign up, and it never asks for your name, email or phone number. The following **stay on your device only** and are never available to the developer:

- **Journal entries, mood check-ins, questionnaire results** (such as PHQ-9 and GAD-7), and game and practice history.
- **Conversations with your companion.** On devices that support Apple Intelligence, replies are generated on your device by Apple's on-device model, and your words never reach the network. On other devices the App uses built-in text.
- **Crisis detection.** If something you write suggests you may be in danger, the App recognises this on your device and shows you where to get help. This happens entirely on your phone. **No one is notified and nothing is sent anywhere.**
- **Health data.** With your permission, the App reads heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, steps and cycle tracking from Apple Health to show your own patterns over time, and may save breathing sessions to Health as Mindful Minutes. Health data **never leaves your device, is never used for advertising or marketing, is never sold, and is never shared with any third party.** You can turn this off at any time in iPhone Settings ▸ Health ▸ Data Access & Devices.
- **Your trusted people.** Names and phone numbers you enter stay on your device and are only used to place a call when you tap to call.
- **Friend invites.** A friend invite is a link you send yourself through the system share sheet (for example, Messages). It contains only the name you choose to show, your inner child, a random invite code, and a chat "public key" (it can only lock messages for you, never open anything). The App has no server and never handles or stores the link.
- **The lock.** The PIN you set is stored as a hash in this device's Keychain. The developer cannot see it, and it is never uploaded. Face ID or Touch ID is verified only on this device.
- **Notifications.** Reminders such as the daily message are local notifications scheduled on your device. They do not go through any server.
- **Apple Watch and widgets.** Data moves directly between your iPhone and Apple Watch through Apple's WatchConnectivity. Widgets read data on the same device only. Neither collects any data.

Apart from instant delivery for friend chat in section 2e, which you turn on yourself, the App does not use iCloud sync. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, the App's on-device data is included in **your own** backup, like any other app's. Apple keeps that backup under your account settings, and the developer cannot access it.

### 2. Data we collect: subscription purchase records

Paid plans are purchased through the Apple App Store, and subscription status is managed by a third-party service, **RevenueCat, Inc.** RevenueCat processes:

- a **randomly generated anonymous ID** (not your name, email or Apple ID);
- the plan you bought, purchase and expiry dates, renewal or refund status, and your App Store country or region and currency;
- technical information needed to run the service, such as App version, operating system version and the IP address of the connection.

This data is used **only** to check whether you are subscribed, unlock paid features, restore purchases, and understand overall subscription numbers (for example, how many people subscribe). It is **not linked to your identity, not used to track you, and not used for advertising.**

Payment itself is handled by Apple. Neither we nor RevenueCat can see your card or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

### 2b. Weather (off by default)

Weather lets your companion know what it is like outside: rain on the window when it rains, a gentle note when it turns cold. It is off by default. While it is off, the App does not access your location and does not look up the weather.

- **When anything is sent:** only after you choose "Approximate location" or a city in Settings ▸ Weather.
- **What is sent:** with "Approximate location", and with your permission, the App asks iOS once for your **approximate location** (not your precise location) and rounds it on your phone to about 10 km before sending it. The original coordinates never leave your phone and are not stored. With a city, only the coordinates of that city's centre are sent.
- **Who processes it:** the App asks Apple directly through **Apple WeatherKit**, built into iOS. This request **does not pass through us** and we cannot see it. Apple handles it under Apple's privacy policy. The list of weather data sources is at https://developer.apple.com/weatherkit/data-source-attribution/
- **What is kept:** your phone keeps only the latest weather reading (conditions, temperature and forecast, no location). It is deleted when you turn Weather off.

You can also withdraw location permission at any time in iPhone Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Location Services.

### 2c. Face follow (the camera is used only when you open it)

Face follow uses the front camera (TrueDepth) to read your face so the companion on screen can turn, blink and open its mouth with you. The camera turns on only while that screen is open.

- **What is read:** this moment's face motion (head turn, blink, jaw), converted in memory into the character's skeleton parameters.
- **Not saved, not sent:** face data is not written to the journal, not included in backups, and not sent to any server. Closing the screen stops the camera at once.
- **Who processes it:** all of it happens on your iPhone, through Apple's ARKit. The developer never sees your face.

### 2e. Instant delivery for friend chat (off by default)

Friend chat normally travels as a link you share yourself. With instant delivery on, messages, gifts and photos go through **Apple's iCloud** straight to your friend instead. They do not pass through us, because the App has no server.

- **Locked on your phone first:** each phone has its own key, kept only in that phone's Keychain, never in iCloud or backups. Once you and a friend have exchanged an invite or a message, both phones work out the same "pair key". Message text, your name, your inner child's name and photos are locked with it on your phone before upload. **Neither we nor Apple can open them.**
- **Where they are kept:** locked messages go into a "mailbox" in the App's iCloud public database, so they **do not use your iCloud storage** and still go through when your iCloud is full. The mailbox number is derived from your pair key, so only the two of you can find it; anyone else who came across it would see only data they cannot open. Letters stay for at most 14 days, then the sender's App takes them back.
- **Doorbell note:** so your friend also gets your public key, the App leaves a "doorbell note" in the public database with your invite code, name and public key. The note is locked with your friend's public key so only they can open it, and its location is derived from their public key, so no one who has not had their invite link can find it.
- **What is not locked:** each letter in the mailbox shows only the mailbox number and the time it was sent, used to find it. Who sent it, what it says, the gift, the photo and your inner child are all inside the locked part.
- **Friends who have not exchanged keys yet:** if a friend's older version of the App has no public key, messages go into an "outbox" in your own iCloud instead, shared with that one friend only. Text is stored in iCloud's own encrypted fields, kept by Apple under your iCloud settings; your invite code, the time, the kind of gift and your inner child's animal are not encrypted. This path uses your own iCloud storage. Photos are only ever sent once keys have been exchanged.
- **Photos:** before sending, photos are resized and re-compressed on your phone; location, camera and capture-time details are not carried over. Photos you receive are kept only on this device and are excluded from backups.
- **Notifications:** a new-message notification only says that a friend sent a message, never who or what.
- **Turning it off or deleting:** turning instant delivery off, blocking a friend, or using Delete everything takes back your letters in the mailbox, your outboxes and the doorbell notes you left. Messages already received stay on your friend's phone. Delete everything also replaces this phone's chat key.
